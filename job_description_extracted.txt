AI Systems & Automation Specialist 
Remote | Full-Time | South America 
About the Role 
We’re looking for a hands-on AI Systems & Automation Specialist to help a growing U.S.-based law firm 
improve how work gets done through AI, automation, and better-connected systems. This is an implementation-
focused role for someone who can understand existing workflows, spot opportunities for improvement, and turn 
ideas into reliable, practical solutions. 
You’ll work closely with firm leadership and team members across administration, legal support, and operations. 
You’ll first learn how the firm currently works, then help design, connect, test, and improve AI -enabled 
workflows. The goal is to help the team work more efficiently and make better decisions usin g accurate 
information and measurable results. 
What You’ll Do 
AI Implementation & Workflow Automation 
 Identify repetitive, manual, or time-consuming workflows that could be improved with AI or automation. 
 Build, configure, test, and refine AI-enabled workflows across the firm’s day-to-day operations. 
 Help connect existing AI capabilities and business systems so information and tasks move more smoothly 
between tools. 
 Evaluate automation opportunities from intake and scheduling through internal administration , reporting, and 
legal-support workflows. 
 Keep people involved in reviewing AI-generated work and ensure that automations are practical, reliable, 
and appropriate for the workflow. 
Systems Integration & Process Improvement 
 Learn the firm’s current tools, processes, and operating procedures before recommending changes. 
 Help integrate or coordinate tools such as Clio, Claude Enterprise, Westlaw AI, Lexis AI, RingCentral, and 
Microsoft SharePoint, subject to access and implementation plans. 
 Work with team members to document workflows, clarify handoffs, and identify gaps, duplication, and 
bottlenecks. 
 Support the development of repeatable systems and documented procedures that can scale as the firm 
grows. 
 Troubleshoot issues, test changes, and make iterative improvements rather than building systems and 
leaving them unsupported. 
Data, Metrics & Reporting 
 Help organize operational data from approved systems and identify ways to improve data quality and 
consistency. 
 Build or improve spreadsheets, dashboards, KPI scorecards, charts, and management reports. 
 Help measure lead sources, response times, consultation bookings and attendance, scheduling issues, 
workflow turnaround times, and other agreed performance indicators. 
 Reconcile data across systems, flag missing or inconsistent information, and clearly document assumptions 
and limitations. 
 Translate findings into concise, understandable insights and practical recommendations for leadership.

Intake, Scheduling & Operational Workflows 
 Help improve the flow of prospective-client inquiries from referral, marketing, and answering-service 
channels into the firm’s intake process. 
 Identify opportunities to make calendar management, appointment confirmations, rescheduling, reminders, 
and record accuracy more consistent. 
 Support better visibility into lead handoffs, intake status, consultation-fee follow-up, and unresolved 
operational issues through approved systems and workflows. 
 Coordinate with the relevant team members and external partners when mapping or improving these 
processes. 
Team Collaboration & Continuous Improvement 
 Participate in daily team meetings and learn the firm’s workflows, priorities, and operating expectations.  
 Work closely with the executive assistant/administrative lead, legal support team, and other c olleagues to 
understand needs and implement solutions. 
 Communicate progress, blockers, risks, and results clearly; take ownership of assigned projects and follow 
through. 
 Protect confidential client, prospective-client, employee, and business information, and follow approved 
access and data-handling procedures. 
What We’re Looking For 
 Hands-on experience implementing AI tools, automations, or connected business workflows —not just using 
AI for basic prompts. 
 Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to understand a process, identify the underlying issue, and build 
a workable solution. 
 Experience connecting systems, configuring automations, or improving how information moves between 
platforms. 
 Comfort working with business data, spreadsheets, dashboards, or operational reporting; strong Excel skills 
are an advantage. 
 Ability to learn new tools quickly, troubleshoot independently, test solutions carefully, and document what 
has been built. 
 Clear written and spoken communication, with the ability to explain technical ideas to nontechnical 
colleagues. 
 A proactive, reliable, detail-oriented approach and comfort working in a fast-moving environment where 
priorities may evolve. 
 Good judgment around confidentiality, data access, accuracy, and human review of AI -generated outputs. 
Helpful Experience 
 Experience with Claude or Claude Enterprise and business-oriented AI implementations. 
 Experience with Clio or other legal practice management platforms. 
 Familiarity with legal research AI tools such as Westlaw AI or Lexis AI. 
 Experience with RingCentral, Microsoft SharePoint, CRM platforms, calendar systems, or intake and 
scheduling workflows. 
 Background in operations, systems administration, analytics, process improvement, or automation 
consulting. 
Work Schedule & Compensation 
 Location: Remote, based in South America.

What Success Looks Like 
 You understand the firm’s existing workflows and can identify high-value opportunities for AI and 
automation. 
 Useful automations and integrations are implemented, tested, documented, and improved over time. 
 The team spends less time on avoidable manual work and has clearer, more reliable processes.

Important: This is an operational technology role, not a legal-advice position. AI-generated outputs and workflow 
changes must be reviewed by the appropriate team members, and legal, accounting, or other restricted 
decisions remain with authorized personnel.
